The system's stocks and the flows between them, discovered from the metrics and kept as a new version each time they change. Machines are linked to flows only as hypotheses, from their events.
more about this pageThere is one model. Stocks are measured quantities (each bound to a series in the metrics store), or hidden ones whose level is what their counters imply; flows move material between them with equations fitted from the history, and machines (programs, AI agents and people, known by their events) are associated with flows only as hypotheses, from their events matching a flow's counter. A flow may have several, or none yet; the associations never change an equation. Every equation says whether it was fitted or only declared.
