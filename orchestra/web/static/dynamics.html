The system dynamics model of the system Orchestra covers. There is one model; it changes over time, and each change is kept as a version. Stocks are measured quantities (each bound to a series in the metrics store), flows move material between them with equations fitted from the history, and machines (AI agents and programs) are attributed to the flows they are believed to cause. Every equation says whether it was fitted or only declared.
