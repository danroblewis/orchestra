An instrument measures the running system and never acts on it: a script Orchestra runs, a URL it reads, or another metrics store it reads. Click one for its series, each with its latest value, and its log.
more about this pageInstruments are not machines: their series are labelled instrument=<name>, which says who measured them, never who did the work. Machines are on the Machines page, known by their events on the bus. Measures machine names the declared machines an instrument is attached to: the owner's statement of which series measure which machine, never a machine made from a series.
