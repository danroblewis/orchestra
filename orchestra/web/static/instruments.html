An instrument measures the running system and never acts on it: a script Orchestra runs ([[collect]]), a URL it reads ([[scrape]]), or another store it reads at each discovery ([[sources]]). Instruments are not machines: their series are labelled instrument=<name>, which says who measured them, never who did the work. Machines are on the Machines page, known by their events on the bus. Hung on names the declared machines an instrument is attached to: the owner's statement of which series measure which machine, never a machine made from a series. Click an instrument for its log.
