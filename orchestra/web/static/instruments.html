An instrument measures the running system and never acts on it: a script Orchestra runs ([[collect]]), a URL it reads ([[scrape]]), or another store it reads at each discovery ([[sources]]). Instruments are not machines: their series are labelled instrument=<name>, which says who measured them, never who did the work. Machines are on the Machines page, known by their events on the bus. Click an instrument for its log.
