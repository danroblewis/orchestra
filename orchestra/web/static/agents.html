Who is in this project and what they are for; the Dashboard shows what they are doing right now. A role is a job description. An agent is an instance of roles in a team and is what runs: continuous agents wake on events, scheduled agents on an interval or a daily time, ad hoc agents once with a brief from their lead. A team has one lead, responsibilities it keeps true, access, and a space in the repository. All of it is created in conversation with the Supervisor and stored under .orchestra/.
