Ladder Glossary Research Editor

Every strategy on every ladder case and variant. Pass rate: the share of runs where every true flow was recovered with the right kind and a rate within tolerance, and nothing spurious. Equivalent: the same linear system with a different structure. Recall / precision: true flows found / found flows that are true. Skill@20: the share of the true model's movement over 20 steps the found model predicts (100% = same as the truth; each run clipped at -100%), on the run it saw and on a fresh run. Complexity of a model = flows + the numbers needed to write its rates over the run (each piece of a rate that changes counts, so an inflow in 9 pieces is 9 numbers; plus effects' numbers, co-flows and hidden stocks), counted the same way for the truth and the found model. Extra: found − true, averaged over runs that found a model (hover for where it comes from); × truth: found ÷ true, the geometric mean. Occam score: accuracy discounted by excess complexity, per run fit × parsimony, where fit is 1 for a pass or equivalent, else the share of true numbers right, and parsimony = true ÷ found (at most 1); averaged over the runs it made (error, timeout = 0; not run yet = left out). Like an information criterion it charges for every number beyond what the truth needs: a model twice as complicated as the truth keeps half its credit, one 10% over keeps 91%; being simpler than the truth earns nothing. Long run (both models run free from the start, no re-anchoring, to t = 100,000; per run capped at 100%, a run that diverges counts 100%): err@100k the gap at t = 100,000 as a share of the truth's range; eq err the settled levels' error; τ err the slowest time constant's relative error; diverges the share of runs that run away, die out or are unstable where the truth is not. Click a strategy's run in the ladder for its line graphs. Strict pass (beside pass, never replacing it): a pass that also has no more complexity than the truth, and a long-run error at 10 × the truth's slowest time constant and a fresh-run skill@100 as good as the truth's own structure refitted on the same kind of data achieves (mean over 7 refits ± 3 standard deviations; never stricter than the truth with every number 2% off). strict pass is over the cells with a verdict; strict known is the share of the runs with one (a pass whose long run or reference is not computed yet has none, and is not counted as a fail). Hover for why passes fail strict. Realistic examples (the right-hand columns, whatever set is picked): SD Lab's 31 example models (sdlab/examples/sd-*) at the two lengths and with the data and seeds of the examples' bench; there behaviour is the headline: real skill@20 is the mean skill@20 over the runs graded (each clipped at −100%, as on the bench; hover for its 95% interval), goals met the runs with skill@20 ≥ 90% and skill@100 ≥ 80% (an error or timeout never meets it) out of all realistic runs, real time the strategy's own time on them (timeouts at 300 s not included; hover for the slowest run and the timeouts). Real data (the last columns, in no set and never in a pass rate): measured series with a published model (BLS labour flows, Gause's lab populations, the 1978 boarding-school flu, US technology adoption; tag real). Discovery sees the first 75% of each series. held-out: skill against persistence on the rest (the found model run free from the last sample it saw; 100% = exact, 0 = no better than holding the last level; each run clipped at −100%; hover for the published model's skill on the same runs); ≥ published: runs that forecast at least as well as the published model refitted on the same samples; agreement: the share of the published structure's parts the found model has (hover for precision): agreement, not a pass; BLS flows: of BLS's six measured gross flows, those found within 25%; twin: on the published model simulated at the data's length and noise, the share of runs that pass or are equivalent, which tells a method failure from a data limit. Physics (the last columns, whatever set is picked; never in the tests' pass rate): 7 physical systems that are not stock-and-flow models (a draining tank, two coupled tanks, Newton cooling, a mass-spring-damper, predator-prey, a reaction chain, a thermostat), solved in continuous time and sampled. phys pass: the coupling graph and signs agree with the physics and the fresh-run skill@20 is within 5 points of the best SD model's (the reference); structure: runs whose coupling graph and signs agree; couplings: the mean share of the physics' couplings found with the right sign; shape: the mean share with the physics' shape (a sqrt or a switch SD Lab's forms cannot write); vs ref @20: fresh skill@20 minus the reference's (0 = as good as the best SD model); long-run err: the found model run free for 5,000 steps against the physics (hover: the reference's). The Set picker chooses the cases the other columns are over (default: the tests). Click a column to sort; hover a column's name for its meaning, and ⓘ beside a strategy for how it works.
