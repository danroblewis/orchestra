🪜 Ladder 🏆 Leaderboard

Each sweep varies one mathematical quantity along a grid, everything else fixed. For each point: every leading strategy's relative error on each true number, against the Cramér–Rao bound (the best standard error any unbiased method can reach on that run) and the scorer's tolerance. Shaded points are where the data cannot deliver a pass (red: unidentifiable, amber: data-limited). A method that fails where the bound is far inside the tolerance is at fault; one that fails where the band is shaded is not. (sdlab-ladder analyse, docs/LADDER.md "Cases as mathematical probes".)
