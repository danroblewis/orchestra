## Why 300 failed broadly

300's low rate came entirely from its two meas variants: without them, 40 of 48 strategies already
passed every none/counts cell of both cases. 300 is the only one of the two with meas variants. Three
things stacked:

1. **Errors in variables (the strategies' fault, 79 cells).** Every one-step least-squares fit
   regresses `inflight[i] - inflight[i-1]` on `traffic[i]` and `inflight[i-1]`. With measurement
   error the error at `i-1` sits in the regressor and, with the opposite sign, in the change, so the
   drain comes out too fast and the input coefficient follows to keep the steady state: 1.221 / 0.612
   on long and 1.275 / 0.637 on short (true 1 / 0.5), 22-27% off with the ratio right. The bias does
   not shrink with run length. **Not the data's limit (rule 4b):** a Kalman likelihood of the true
   structure on the same data gives 1.0045 / 0.5049 (long) and 1.019 / 0.511 (short), with 3 x SE of
   6.8% and 16.2%; odefit (an output-error fit) passed both. In combined2, engine_forms and
   kalman_lna (6 cells) a second fault stacked on top: their Kalman path takes over under measurement
   error and never reads measured inputs, giving a runaway `2.087 x inflight` out with a spurious
   `2.064 x inflight` link in.
2. **The harness's noise oracle ignored measured inputs (0 cells, but dishonest tolerances).** The
   clean counts view dropped `inputs`, so meas cells got no standard errors and a bare 10% tolerance,
   and `noise_kf.SSM` built its system over the stocks only, so `show 300 --variant meas-*` crashed
   (`TypeError: cannot unpack non-iterable NoneType object`). Predicted, and confirmed, to flip no cell.
3. **Strategies that crash on or ignore the input (none/counts cells too).** See the next section;
   on 300 these were 40 numopt cells, 12 common_cause/sindy cells and 17 crashes across both cases.

## Why anything failed 301

Every 301 failure (30 cells) was a strategy mishandling the measured input `traffic`:

- **numopt_bo/cma/nm/powell (16):** the pinned `outerror.Objective` builds `Model(flows, ids)` with no
  `inputs=` and `numopt.identify_with` judges without them, so the refit (queue -> inflight 0.0003-0.004
  for a true 0.6) was judged on the same blind simulation and kept.
- **common_cause and sindy (8):** the input is not a column of their regressions, so traffic was
  absorbed as outside-inflow pieces and the drains biased, with spurious `inflight -> queue` terms.
- **shared_noise (4) and periodic_inflows (2, long):** crash when a matrix row's coefficients name the
  input (`KeyError: 'traffic'`, `'traffic' is not in list`): prev was built from the stocks only.

After those fixes one non-pass remained: shared_noise on 301 none-short and none-long, **equivalent**
(the move `queue -> inflight 0.6 x queue` read as `queue -> outside` plus a link `outside -> inflight`,
exact behaviour, recall 2/3, complexity 9 vs 7). The scorer was right; the fault was shared_noise's
move-or-link test on noise-free residuals (round 3 below).

## Retirements and the skeptics' arguments

**Retired: none.** `sdlab/ladder/retired.yaml` is unchanged.

- **shared_noise, proposed for retirement and refuted.** The case for: its pass rate (294 of 1,435
  paired cells) is below matrix (426) and far below combined2 (904), and kalman_bayes covers all but
  15 of its 370 passes. The case against, which held: its 300/301 failure was a fixable method fault
  (`sdlab-ladder analyse` rates both 301 none variants achievable, p 1.00), and 2212020 fixed it with
  no pass lost; it is the only strategy that tells moves from links and finds co-flows from shared
  counting noise, and it has ~37-39 cells no other strategy passes. Keep it as a component.
- **"300 is just too noisy" (refuted).** The Kalman bound on the same data labels both meas variants
  achievable (3 x SE 6.8% long, 16.2% short) and odefit passed; the strategies' 22-27% error was the
  estimator, so the fix went into the strategies, not the tolerance.
- **"Fixing the oracle will pass the meas cells" (refuted).** The oracle fix widened meas-short only to
  the measured 16.9% and flipped 0 of 12,128 cells; least squares was 27.5% off.
- **"The numopt objective needs fixing in the shared library" (declined).** The library is pinned;
  each numopt package carries its own copy instead.
