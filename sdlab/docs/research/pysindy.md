# PySINDy with SD-shaped libraries and conservation constraints

**Status:** running (wave 2, benchmark v2)

## Hypothesis
Discrete-time PySINDy with a library of levels and pairwise products (a product A x C in a flow out of
A is a linear effect of C), outside inflows as control inputs or our PELT pieces, and conservation as
equality constraints (ConstrainedSR3), recovers structure better than stepwise BIC, especially effects.
Plain PySINDy as a reference.
