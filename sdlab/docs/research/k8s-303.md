## Why strategies failed 303

The new feature (smooth daily traffic) triggered the counts-short failures and part of the meas
failures. The meas failures' main cause is older: 303 is the first case on the ladder with level-scaled
measurement error on a final cumulative counter. Rung 302 had no meas variant, and rung 300 had meas but
no counter.

**C1. The counter's row is buried by measurement error (meas, 97-98 cells, every strategy). Older
cause.** The counter reaches 1.03-1.25e5 (short) and 7.3-8.1e5 (long). At 5% error, its step changes
carry noise sd 4,305 and 30,232, against a true flow of about 345-400 a step. In change space,
inflight's coefficient is 0.49 ± 0.29 (short) and 0.53 ± 0.84 (long), so stepwise selection leaves
the counter's row empty, and inflight's drain goes to `outside`. In level space the same data pin it at
0.498 / 0.502. The judge would accept the move (matrix line: 0.327 rerouted against 0.375 chosen), but
the move was never offered. A counterfactual check settles the cause: 302's step traffic with the meas
variants added in process fails in the same way (lna `inflight -> outside 0.5076`, no counter flow).

**C2. Smooth exact traffic stands in for the noisy queue (meas, all 98 cells). The new feature
together with an older weakness.** Traffic is exact, and as a smooth cycle it is almost a copy of the
true queue. In inflight's row, least squares gives traffic the queue's job
(queue/inflight/traffic = 0.33/-0.74/+0.93 on short), which adds a spurious `outside -> inflight =
k x traffic` and inflates the drain to 0.68-0.89. On the pinned main line, the same bias splits the
queue's drain into `queue -> outside` plus a small move. On meas-long the matrix line added 33 chance
pieces on inflight. eiv.py's instrumented refit never changed structure, and its multi-step judge is
itself biased by measurement error at short horizons (truth 0.3769 against 0.3745 for the biased
answer). Rung 300 meas, with step-like traffic, passed.

**C3 / D1. Chance pieces on the final counter (counts-short, 8 cells: coflow_hats, effect_hats,
held_out, inflow_effects, loop_transit, realistic_flows, realistic_hats, sindy). The new feature
triggers an older fault.** All 8 returned the pinned main line's answer unchanged: the 3 true parts 6-8%
high (move 0.6375, drain 0.5402, traffic 1.064), plus a spurious `responses_total -> outside 30.3` in 5
pieces. The daily cycle raises corr(queue, traffic) from 0.961 (302) to 0.9925. That roughly doubles
the row SEs, so inflight's own row reads the drain as 0.5417 ± 0.027, against the counter's 0.5017 ±
0.0014. The leftover follows the daily cycle and ends up as outflow pieces from the counter, which the
in-sample judge does not price. This is 302's cause A one stock further along. None of the 8 had
received 302's earned refit (8c507a8), because they had passed 302 without it.

**C2r / D2 / D3. Three leftovers after rounds 1-2 (meas).** sindy and symbolic_anchor meas-short: broken
start structures (traffic into inflight, no queue row) that eiv's pruning guard keeps. odefit meas-long:
odefit's own choice picks its output-error model (held-out edge about 1%), whose structure is wrong.

**H1. Harness (no failing cells).** `sync --strategies all` skipped parked strategies that are still
active (numopt_bo, numopt_cma, numopt_nm, numopt_powell). Their 24 rows on 303 were from before the
earned refit and showed 6 false counts failures. Separately, `sdlab-ladder analyse` crashed on meas
variants (KeyError 'crb_se').

## Round 1: fixes and flips

**H1 (be34a8a, harness).** `select_strategies(spec, parked_too=False)`, and `sync` passes
`parked_too=True`, so `sync --strategies all` now covers parked strategies that are still active
(retired ones stay excluded). analysis_report.py falls back to `se_kalman`. A new test is
tests/test_ladder_sync_parked.py. No scoring change: the score hash stayed ec821eb6c32637e1, so 0 flips
(all 294 303 cells were re-scored: 188 pass->pass, 106 fail->fail). The claim that the meas tolerance is
about 30x too wide was **refuted as a meas issue**. The meas/counts ratio is 1.50-1.84, which is the
information actually lost. The 30x gap is already present in counts: the per-stock least-squares oracle
cannot see conservation. Narrowing that would change what a pass means across the whole ladder, so it is
left for the user (see below).

**C2 (def0acb, eiv.py in all 48 copies plus a new odefit/eiv.py; docs 794beba).** This applies only
where the white test shows measurement error. Each part gets a Wald test on the instrumented fit
(sandwich covariance, 2-step Bartlett), priced at Akaike's 2 per number. The weakest part is dropped and
the rest refitted. Rows touched by level outliers are left out. Guard: keep the pruned model if, at the
judge's longest horizon, it is within 2 paired SE of the found model.
- Rungs 300-303, 980 cells: 874 pass->pass, 106 fail->fail, **0 flips**. No 303 meas cell could pass
  yet because of C1.
- C2 symptoms on the 98 meas cells: traffic link into inflight 98 -> 3, split queue drain 56 -> 1,
  chance pieces on inflight 42 -> 1.
- Paired sample of 2,744 cells (080-084, 087, 139, 142; before = pruning switched off in process):
  fail->pass 58, fail->equivalent 100, pass->fail 1 (realistic_flows 087 meas-short), equivalent->fail
  1 (kalman_bayes 082 outliers-short). Summed run time +6.5%.
- Rejected options: Bayes' price (1 of 49 short cells right, against 47 with Akaike's); the full judge
  as the guard (it rejects every pruning that drops pieces); Hansen's J test (no power on smooth data).

## Round 2: fixes and flips

**C1 (9956255, with fix b9fe1d6; docs 49e4ca7, k8s-303-c1.md).** The change is in eiv.py, all 49 copies
(40 identical files, 9 inline copies). It runs only where the white test shows measurement error, after
the refit and pruning. Each drain `X -> outside` is tried as a move `X -> R` into every other measured
stock. The test uses R's row in level space: R's measured level minus the cumulative sum of its found
flows, with a free start. The noise variance is a + b x level², fitted by feasible GLS, with squared
residuals capped at 25 variances. The move is made when R's -2 log-likelihood falls by more than
Bayes' price per added number. A second candidate replaces an outside inflow into R that reads X. This
is a structure change inside one fit, not a selection, and there is no branching on case, variant,
final stock or input type. b9fe1d6 fixed the inline generator: in 9956255 the move never ran in the 9
inline copies, and no 9956255 rows are quoted.
- Rungs 300-303, 980 cells: 874 pass->pass, **95 fail->pass** (303 meas), 11 fail->fail, **0
  pass->fail**.
- Paired: 080-087 x 8 noisy variants (2,352 cells) 0 flips. 139/142/143 every variant (588 cells): 34
  fail->pass and 1 fail->equivalent, all on 143 meas-long and confirmed off/on in process. The 3 other
  flips there were against older rows, where the move cannot run. **0 regressions.**
- Left open: the drain keeps its instrumented number (0.39 short, 0.46 long) and is not re-estimated
  jointly with the level-space row. 13 strategies still fail 143 meas-long (pieces fill the receiver's
  row).

independent skeptics who tried to refute it with the store and their own runs.

**symbolic_anchor: retired (confirmed; neither skeptic refuted it).**
- Skeptic 1 (coverage): on the latest rows, counting pass or equivalent among active strategies, it
  passes 651 cells and is the sole passer of none. Its thinnest cells are phys-lotka-volterra meas-long
  (matrix_v2 also passes) and meas-short (sindy and odefit also pass). matrix_v2 covers all but 3 of its
  cells, and those 3 have other passers. Against matrix: 9 wins, 5 losses, and every win is shared.

  variants (`also: []`, the best variant in symbolic-addition-judge.md) gave identical statuses on 13
