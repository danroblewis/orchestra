## Diagnosis (rule 4b)

Every 303 meas-short and meas-long cell (49 + 49) had no flow into `responses_total`: inflight's drain was
scored as a spurious `inflight -> outside`. `sdlab-ladder analyse` rates both variants achievable (p 1.00),
so this is the method's fault.

5% error sits on a level that grows to 103k (short) or 725k (long), so the counter's step changes have sd
4,305 and 30,232 against a signal of about 345 a step. In change space the counter's row cannot pay for
`0.5 x inflight` (t 1.68 short, 0.63 long; BIC gains 2.81 and 0.40 below log T), so stepwise selection leaves
it empty and the drain has no receiver. The level keeps what came in: in level space (the counter's level
against cumulative sums, weighted by the measured level) inflight is the clear best column and estimates
0.4976 / 0.4973. The same failure appears on 302's step traffic once meas variants are added in process, so
it is not the rung's feature; 303 is the first case with measurement error on a final counter.

## The change (eiv.py, all 49 copies; 9956255 and b9fe1d6)

Only where eiv.py's white test already shows measurement error, after the refit and pruning:

1. **R's row in level space.** For each measured stock R: its measured level less the cumulative sum of
   what the found flows (and announced injections, added before their step's flows) add to it, from the
   measured levels; a free starting level; noise of variance `a + b x level^2`, fitted from the residuals
   by feasible GLS on the measured level (not unweighted per-step changes), each squared residual counted at
   most TRIM^2 = 25 variances (outliers).
2. **A drain as a move.** Each drain `X -> outside` is tried as `X -> R` for every other measured stock R,
   at the drain's own number; and, where R takes an inflow from outside that reads X (proportional on X, or
   a linear term on X), as the move replacing that reading.
3. **The price.** The best candidate is made when R's row -2 log-likelihood drops by more than log n plus
   Bayes' log n for each number it adds (one for choosing the receiver, less one per number it replaces);
   repeat while one does. A move that duplicates a proportional move already there is joined to it.

No step reads the case, the variant, final stocks or the kind of input; it is a change of structure inside
one fit (the refit), not a choice between finished models. The drain number is kept (no level-space
re-estimate): the prototype showed the move alone suffices to pass. b9fe1d6 only renames `_refit` to
`_refit_numbers`: the inline generator mapped both `refit` and `_refit` to `_eiv_refit`, so at 9956255 the
9 inline copies never ran the move (no rows of 9956255 are quoted below).

Rejected on the way (scratchpad `c1r2/merge.py`, on the stored def0acb flows): the first prototype ignored
injections in the level row, and on 087 (injections into A) it moved a spurious `A -> outside 0.03` into B
in 30 cells (all stayed pass) and turned censored 087 meas-long equivalent -> fail. Adding injections before
their step's flows removed every 087 fire. Ungated over all 24 non-303 cases with meas variants, the
prototype changed no passing cell and turned 47 143 meas-long cells fail -> pass (the same cause: a chain
ending in a measured C).
