## Diagnosis (rule 4b)

`sdlab-ladder analyse 303-k8s-daily-traffic` rates both meas variants achievable (p 1.00), so the
failures are the method's.

**Inflight's row.** Traffic is exact and, being smooth, is almost a copy of the true queue. Least squares
on each step's change in inflight therefore gives traffic the queue's job (scratchpad `iv1.py`, `iv3.py`
over the cell's data):

| inflight row, regressors | short | long |
|---|---|---|
| least squares [queue, inflight] | 0.634 / -0.531 | 0.586 / -0.489 |
| least squares [queue, inflight, traffic] | 0.33 / -0.74 / **+0.93** | 0.27 / -0.73 / **+1.02** |
| instrumented (levels 2 steps back) [queue, inflight, traffic] | 0.35 / -0.46 / +0.34 (t 0.5) | 0.50 / -0.44 / **+0.04** (t 0.2) |
| move pinned at 0.6, least squares [inflight, traffic] | -0.744 / +0.485 | -0.731 / +0.463 |
| move pinned at 0.6, instrumented [inflight, traffic] | -0.515 / +0.027 | -0.450 / -0.100 |
| move pinned at 0.6, [inflight] alone | 0.503 (both fits) | 0.50 (both fits) |

The instrumented fit cannot tell the traffic link from nothing; least squares keeps it, and the drain
inflates to keep the steady state. Forward and backward selection both run on least squares, so both
keep it. On the pinned main line (lna and the families built on it) the queue's row is biased the same
way (traffic 1.30-1.37, queue -0.78/-0.82), so the backward candidates split the queue's drain into
`queue -> outside` 0.43-0.59 plus a move of 0.22-0.34. On meas-long the matrix line (earned refit) adds
33 chance pieces on inflight. eiv.py's refit kept the structure, so none of this could change.

**Why eiv.py's guard kept the biased numbers.** On 303 meas-short, eiv's instrumented refit of matrix's
answer was rejected by its own guard: judge 0.3791 against 0.3747 for the found numbers. The judge (the
mean multi-step error over 5, 20 and 100 steps, from the measured levels) prefers the biased answers to
the truth itself (scratchpad `judges.py`, `dbg3.py`):

| model | short | long |
|---|---|---|
| the truth (1, 0.6, 0.5) | 0.3769 | 0.2134 |
| matrix's found (with the 0.35 traffic link) | 0.3745 | 0.2124 |
| least squares numbers on the true structure | 0.3745 | 0.2112 |
| instrumented numbers on the true structure | 0.3846 | 0.2144 |
| matrix's found on long, with its 33 pieces | - | 0.2039 |

All of the gap is at the 5-step horizon (inflight 2.27 vs 2.36 for the truth on long): each run starts
from a level with 5% error in it, and a faster-draining model forgets that error sooner. At 100 steps
the models are tied (0.0181 both). So eiv.py's claim that this judge is not biased by measurement error
is wrong at its short horizons, and an in-sample judge puts no price on 33 pieces either.

**The daily cycle is the trigger.** Rung 300-302 traffic is step-like, so the queue's level is not a
smooth function of traffic and the link is not a stand-in; 300 meas passes.

## The change (def0acb)

In eiv.py (all 48 copies: 39 byte-identical `eiv.py` files, 9 inline `_eiv_` copies generated from it,
and a new copy on odefit's main line, which had none), where the white test shows measurement error and
before the old refit:

1. **Prune on the instrumented fit.** Each part (a flow, or one term of a multi-term linear rate; flows
   that follow eras are left alone) is tested by the Wald statistic of its numbers under the
   instrumented fit, with a sandwich covariance (moments summed over the stocks per step, Bartlett
   window of 2 steps, as measurement error leaves them MA(1)). The part with the largest shortfall below
   2 per number (Akaike's price) is dropped and the rest refitted; repeat until every part pays. A part
   that never flows (its rate gone negative) goes first. Rows a level outlier touches (a one-step
   residual beyond 5 robust sds, and the rows it feeds as a change or an instrument) are left out of
   these fits.
2. **Guard at the longest horizon.** The pruned model is kept if, at the judge's longest horizon
   (100 steps on long, 20 on short), it is no further from the data than the found model by more than
   2 paired standard errors over the judge's ~80 runs. Failing that, the previous pruning stage is
   tried, and so on; failing all, the old refit runs as before.

No step reads the case, the variant or the kind of input. It is a change inside one fit (the structure's
own refit), not a choice between finished models. odefit's own choice between its main line and its
output-error model is unchanged; only its main line is refitted before that choice.

Rejected on the way (scratchpad `eivp.py`, the prototype with switches):
- Bayes' price (log n per number): on short the instrumented fit identifies the queue's scale only
  weakly (move and traffic t 2.07 each once the link is gone), so it pruned the whole queue row: with
  inflight's drain rerouted into the counter (the C1 stand-in), 1 of 49 meas-short cells right against
  47 with Akaike's price (both 49 of 49 on meas-long).
- The judge as the guard, at any horizon mix: rejects every pruned model with pieces in the found one
  (3.1-3.5 paired SE at 5 and 20 steps), as above.
- Hansen's J test (lags 2 and 3) as the guard: no power on smooth data (accepted sindy's and
  symbolic_anchor's broken short models, judge 0.51 and 1.00 against 0.35 and 0.63).
