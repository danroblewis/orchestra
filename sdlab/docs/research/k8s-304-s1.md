# Kubernetes 304, cause S1: conservation folds the timeout drain into the move

**Status:** done (2026-10-20). Code commit 7b6d7f4 (effect_hats, inflow_effects, shared_noise). All 3
cells fixed (304 counts-short: equivalent -> pass). On 300-304 the three strategies pass 72/72 cells.
Paired samples show 1 fail->pass elsewhere and 0 pass->fail.

Case 304-k8s-timeouts-and-errors adds `queue -> outside = 0.08 x queue` (timeouts) beside the move
`queue -> inflight = 0.6 x queue`. On counts-short the three strategies dropped the timeout. They
returned the move with traffic's gain lowered instead (effect_hats/inflow_effects: traffic 0.8814; shared_noise:
traffic 1.139 with no drain). These answers were "equivalent", not passes.

## Cause (confirmed)

- **effect_hats, inflow_effects.** The pinned main line's conservation sets the queue's drain to the
  move (0.9094 -> 0.6411, inside the 30% slack for a row with changing inflows). The excess goes to
  7 constant outflow pieces on the queue. The re-cut base's `prune` can only drop those pieces. The
  joint refit then hands their work to traffic's gain (0.8814), and no proportional drain is ever
  offered. Offering `queue -> outside = k x queue` in their place and refitting jointly lowers the
  price from 6544.7 to 6540.0, with queue -> outside 0.1767 and traffic 1.139. That is the answer the
  passing strategies give (coflow_hats: 0.1767 / 1.139).
- **shared_noise.** Its own `matrix.conserve` takes the BIC branch. The forced fit already has one
  number fewer, and the test adds another log T on top, so it allowed up to two log T. On the chosen
  configuration (forward, changing inflows, trust receivers) the queue row's RSS goes from 239212.3 to
  246521.6 over T = 299, which is dBIC +3.3 at log T 5.70. The forced fit was accepted anyway, and the
  0.164 excess drain was lost.

The 304 drain reads about 2x the truth (0.16-0.18 against 0.08) for every strategy. That is seed
304's draw, not a bias (k8s-304-s2.md).

## Changes (each strategy's own package; nothing imported across strategies)

- `effect_hats/price.py` and `inflow_effects/price.py` (identical change): in `prune()`, an outflow
  to outside carried by pieces on a stock X with no `k x X` drain is also tried as that drain in their
  place. It is refitted jointly with every other number (joint.refit) and kept only when the price
  falls (log T per number, as for every flow). No case or input-type branch.
- `shared_noise/matrix.py` `conserve`: the forced fit is priced at one log T (`opts["conserve_allow"]`,
  default 0, in log T; 1 restores the old allowance).
