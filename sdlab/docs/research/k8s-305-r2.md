**Status:** done (2026-10-20), round 1. All 8 cells fixed (305 counts: equivalent -> pass) in effect_hats,
inflow_effects, combined_misfit, combined_r2 and shared_noise. On 300-305 the five strategies pass 140/140

Case 305-k8s-retries is rung 304 with retries: `queue -> retries` 0.08 (timeouts), `retries -> queue` 0.6 and
`retries -> outside` 0.4 (the retries stock drains about all it holds each step).

## Cause (confirmed, with one addition)

- **The pinned main line's conservation leaves a split** (effect_hats, inflow_effects, and the matrix-based
  specialists inside combined_*). The queue's row drains 0.6563 (counts-long), less than its precise receivers
  take (0.6167 + 0.0822). Conservation only raises a drain on a row with a changing inflow, so the excess
  becomes `outside -> retries = 0.0425 x queue` beside `queue -> retries = 0.0396`. The hats' `prune` never
  dropped the link because it moves more than 0.25 x the median flow. The merged model prices lower anyway:
  52101.9 against 52107.6. On counts-short, prune had already dropped the small `inflight -> errors_total`
  move and kept the link `outside -> errors_total = 0.0294 x inflight`. The move in its place prices 0.3
  higher, which is a tie.
- **combined_misfit, combined_r2.** The fit check sends the choice to multi-step error. There the
  matrix-based candidates (censored, effect_hats, hidden_transit, periodic_inflows, all with the split)
  beat lna by 0.187 to 0.188. With the link merged, a candidate's judge score falls from 38286.24 to
  38283.05.
- **shared_noise, an addition to the stated cause: the noise analysis misreads moves out of retries.** The
  retries stock drains 0.96-1.0 of its level per step. The simulator caps outflows at what a stock holds, so
  what leaves retries is its level, not a Poisson draw. Its moves share no draw with their receivers, and
  `noise.analyse` decides "link": `retries -> queue` on counts-long (cost 23), and `queue -> retries` in
  place of it on counts-short (cost 16.8). Conservation then lowered the queue's drain. A second problem
  sat beside it: when `conserve` set a drain, it kept the free fit's other numbers (traffic gain 0.940
  where the forced refit reads 0.98), so the judge preferred the split model (0.197 against 0.311).

## Changes (each strategy's own package; no cross-imports; no case or input-type branch)

- `effect_hats/price.py` = `inflow_effects/price.py` (`prune`, new `_move_for`): a link `outside -> Y =
  k x X` (X a stock, X != Y) is offered as the move `X -> Y`, whatever its size. It is merged into an
  existing move, or put in the link's place. The model is refitted jointly and kept when the price falls.
  The new-move form gets the strategy's own link prior (`prior` x log T, the charge `rank()` already makes
  for a rate that is not its source's level). A merge gets no prior (a26804b). With the prior, the true
  link in 045-random-link#8 counts-short was merged into the move beside it. `__init__` passes the prior
  as `link_prior`.
- Vendored `combined/__init__.py` in combined_misfit and combined_r2 (`choose`, new `_links_as_moves`):
  the same offer for every candidate after its refit. Each alternative is refitted (refit.py) and kept
  while the judge's score falls (`link_moves`, default on). This is a structure step inside one
  candidate's fit, like prune. It is not a choice between finished models.
- `shared_noise/noise.py` (`analyse`, `capped`, default z 2): a source whose drain d satisfies
  d + 2 sqrt(d / mean level) >= 1 is capped. Its moves get no cross-covariance term, so the noise
  leaves them undecided.
- `shared_noise/matrix.py` (`conserve`):
  - Raising a drain to what trusted receivers take also applies to rows with a steady inflow, priced by
    the refit test (`conserve_up`).
  - The forced fit's other numbers are written back (`conserve_refit`).
  - Conservation runs in passes until nothing changes (at most 3).
  - `conserve_follow` (f1069d9) also raised drains in trust-rows configurations where the noise reads
    every receiver as a move. It lifted the fresh seeds further (counts-long 12/12, counts-short 7/12).
    But it broke 301 counts-short: inflight's gain from queue (0.6838) is biased by a spurious traffic
    outflow in inflight's own row, and the queue's traffic gain refits to 1.133. It is **off by
    default** (4a9db19) and kept as an option.

## Open

- Every strategy reads `retries -> queue` at about 0.70 and `retries -> outside` at about 0.25 (true 0.6
  and 0.4). These are inside the tolerance. The total drain is right (about 0.96). The split is biased
  because retries' outflows are capped at its level. Not diagnosed further here.
- shared_noise's trust-rows configurations still split on some fresh seeds. A fix needs receivers whose
  rows have no spurious terms (see the 301 counts-short case for conserve_follow).
- The capped-source rule exists only in shared_noise. lna's likelihood has the same blind spot: lna keeps
  `queue -> inflight` 0.570 + `outside -> inflight` 0.0303 x queue, a split its LNA likelihood prefers by
  27.5 nats. lna is not part of this cause.
