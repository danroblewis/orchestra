## Why strategies failed 302

The new feature (the final counter) triggered both causes. Each one exposed an older weakness.

**A. Chance pieces on inflight beat the constrained row (18 cells in round 1, plus 3 more in round 2
as A2).** matrix and 17 other strategies returned the three true parts within 1.5%. They also returned
`inflight -> outside 32.2` in 4 pieces (steps 2-238: 34.5, 239-243: 2.3, 244-296: 26.8, 297-300: 4.4;
7.6 oracle SE, spurious) and `outside -> inflight 0.0635 x queue` (2.9 SE, scored as noise).
- What is old: the fine PELT pass (0.5 x log n x the noise variance) runs on raw Δinflight and finds
  chance pieces on a stock with no outside inflow. The in-sample judge puts no price on complexity.
- What is new: the counter's row fixes inflight's drain at 0.4973. The leftover drain
  ((0.5382 - 0.4973) x inflight) goes into the pieces, and the pieces candidate wins: 'changing inflows'
  0.0461 against 0.0520 for 'backward, constant inflows'. The constant-inflow candidate would pass.
- Evidence: with responses_total dropped from the same data, the same 4 pieces appear, but inflight's
  drain fits freely (0.5145) and the no-pieces candidate wins (0.04000 vs 0.04003). The counter's own
  share of the judge error is about 0. 301 had no pieces on its chosen candidate.

**B. An input-blind judge picks the counter-only candidate (4 cells: held_out, matrix_v2, recession,
starved_coflows).** Each returned only `outside -> responses_total = 0.4973 x inflight` (recall 0).
- What is new: under counting noise on 300 steps, forward selection adds no column to queue's or
  inflight's row, because traffic and queue are collinear. In 301 that left the 'forward, constant
  inflows' candidate empty (judge inf). In 302 the counter row is one regressor, so the candidate exists,
  with judge 0.987.
- What is old: these four judges stepped candidates without the measured inputs. Blind, every
  candidate that reads traffic scores 17.7-18.7 (0.046-0.052 with inputs), so the degenerate candidate
  wins. k8s-300-301.md had already flagged recession and periodic_inflows for judging blind.
  - recession: `judge(...)` without `inputs=`
  - starved_coflows: the same
  - matrix_v2: `inputs_judge` off by default
  - held_out: the pinned `holdout.held_out_errors` never passes inputs

## Round 1 fixes and flips

**Cause A (8c507a8; docs e25727e).** 17 strategies each got their own copy of earned_pieces' refit
(`earned.py`, `earned_rows.py`, `earned_joint.py` from 66086e7) and call `earned.main_line` where they
called `identify.identify`: matrix, blockwise, censored, drifting_drain, hidden_transit, latent_eras,
mass_balance, master_eq, odefit, own_tables, periodic_inflows, symbolic_anchor, unified and numopt_bo,
cma, nm and powell. Every main-line candidate is rebuilt before the judge: rows are chosen again, pieces
are kept only where earned, and conservation is decided jointly on the counting likelihood.
- One addition in the copies: pieces found again at the textbook price (pelt, mult 2) compete on the same
  BIC. Without it, mass_balance's `one_hidden` main line fell from skill@20 -0.25 to -356 (with it: 0.88).
- shared_noise got only the copied `earned_rows.reselect`, before its own `finish`.
- This is a change inside one fit, not a selection.
- Ladder: `uv run sdlab-ladder run --strategies <the 18> --cases 300,301,302 --jobs 4 --timeout 600`
  gave 252/252 pass. 302 counts-short went fail -> pass for all 18, and no 300/301 cell flipped.
- Paired sample 1 (in process, 18 strategies x 64 cells = 1,152 pairs):
  - 184 fail -> pass and 35 fail -> equivalent
  - lost: master_eq 048#3 counts-long (pass -> fail) and drifting_drain 196 none-long (equivalent -> fail)
- Paired sample 2 (risk-targeted, 113 pairs): 16 pass -> not pass and no gains. 15 of these come from
  earned_pieces' own refit: noise-free collinear cross-links at 042#5 and 043#6/#8, and outliers at 087.
  One (drifting_drain 121 counts-short) comes from the textbook-price addition. Full table: git show
  e25727e:docs/research/k8s-302.md.
- mass_balance's own `one_hidden` test lost its hidden stock (alpha 1.135 > 1.05), so 3 tests are
  `xfail(strict=True)`.

**Cause B (c33de93).** Each of the four judges now steps candidates with `opts['inputs']`.
- recession and starved_coflows: `inputs=` added.
- matrix_v2: `inputs_judge` on by default.
- held_out: its own copy of `holdout.held_out_errors`, which slices the inputs per held-out block.
- With no measured inputs, all four behave exactly as before.
- Ladder: `uv run sdlab-ladder run --strategies held_out,matrix_v2,recession,starved_coflows --cases

## Retirements and the skeptics' arguments

**Retirements: none.** No strategy was proposed for retirement on this rung, and `sdlab/ladder/retired.yaml`
is unchanged. Every failing strategy's fault was fixable inside its own fit. After the fixes, all 49
pass every variant, so 302 dominates no strategy.

These proposals were made during the investigation and refuted:
- **matrix's `gate` option instead of the earned refit** (refuse pieces on rows with no outside
  inflow). It passed 302 for 14 of the 15 strategies tried. On matrix's 134-cell paired sample it went
  +6/-2, against +24/-0 for the earned refit, so it was not taken.
- **Turning on matrix_v2's `white` or `pelt_mult 2`.** Each passes 302, but on the same 94 cells
  `white` went +1/-2 and `pelt_mult 2` went +2/-2, against +4/-0 for `earned`. Not taken.
- **"The leftover 302 failures in matrix_v2, recession and starved_coflows are a second cause"** (round 1,
  cause B). Refuted by review 2: these were cause A in strategies the refit had not reached. Wrapping
  their `identify_one` with the refit made them pass.
- **The input-aware judge as the cause of the 9 cause-B losses.** Refuted: with inputs, the judge ranks
  correctly (200: 0.189 vs 0.239 for a structurally wrong candidate). The fault is candidate generation,
  or judging before the eiv refit.
- **A scoring change.** None was needed or made. The data supports a pass (p 1.00), and the oracle SE
  marks the 32.2 constant as 7.6 SE.
