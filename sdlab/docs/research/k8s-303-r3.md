**Loss found and fixed (26767e0).** In the paired sample, 058-move-equals-other-chains-drain none-long went

## symbolic_anchor, 303 meas-short

**Diagnosis.** Achievable (p 1.00; k8s-303-c2.md). The base main line plus eiv gave the true structure,
but the addition test accepted an effect candidate `inflight x cut(inflight)` with no queue row and traffic
into inflight (judge 8.70 against the base's 0.3747). The pinned `addition.addition_test` builds every
`Model` without the measured inputs. So the base's `outside -> queue = 1 x traffic` ran with traffic at 0
(base error 46.4 in the test), and the coarse re-measured inflow made up for it in the candidate only.
`fit()` also judged each candidate without the inputs. This is a fault in the method's own judge, not a
data limit.

**Change (181ec20).** symbolic_anchor/addition.py is a copy of the pinned addition.py at 49e4ca7 (with
`judges.refit_inflow`). Every run, re-measured inflow and remeasure residual in it reads `inputs`. The
strategy passes `opts["inputs"]` to it and to `judge`. In process, the test now gives base 0.3747 against
cand 0.3727, gain 0.005 < 2 x noise 0.0058, so the effect is rejected. eiv then prunes the traffic link,
moves the drain into the counter, and the cell passes.

## Capability remarks

Coverage evidence comes from `store.latest` over the 44 active strategies (scratchpad `r303b/uniq.py`,
2026-10-20). The board mixes versions: most rows are from 76845d5 to b9fe1d6.

- **coflow_hats:** linear-in-levels rates from the matrix main line (moves, drains, links, measured
  inputs as columns), outside inflows in pooled pieces, plus effects as piecewise-linear tent curves of one
  stock (table effects, MIN/MAX-like saturations), co-flows (a flow that uses another), co-flows whose used
  stock runs dry, and links whose strength follows a cycle. It cannot represent hidden stocks (0/86),
  delays (0/82), gaps or goal-seeking (0/106), control loops (1/84) or general products. Noise is handled
  by counting-likelihood conservation (earned_joint) and the eiv refit for measurement error. Against its
  parent effect_hats it wins 12 cells (128 and 129 co-flows, 132 daily-cycle link, 043#3, 303 cs) and loses 4
  (053 sweep-excitation none). It is the sole passer of no cell: combined2 and realistic_flows also pass
  128, 129 and 132. **Limitation: fixable** (both faults found were in copied machinery and are fixed);
  **not retired.**
- **symbolic_anchor:** the matrix main line (earned) plus effects from the symbolic screen (curves of a
  stock's level), accepted only by the anchored addition test. It represents what matrix does plus, rarely,
  one effect: it passes effect 1/114, and its tag profile matches matrix's (494/1437 against 494/1437
  passes). It wins 3 cells against matrix (017 none-short, phys-lotka-volterra meas-short and meas-long)
  and loses 3 (048#3 cs, 142 meas-long, part-conveyor-bottleneck cl). It is the sole passer of no cell:
  matrix_v2 also passes Lotka-Volterra meas-long, and 18 strategies pass 017. It has no hidden stocks,
  delays, gaps or control loops. Its effect test is structurally conservative: 0 of 44 effect-table cells
  pass. **Limitation: hard** in the sense of rule 4b's coverage. Its one mechanism (effects judged
  against an anchored inflow) adds almost nothing over matrix, and effect_hats, coflow_hats and
  realistic_hats cover effects (10/114). A recommendation to retire is in order, but this round's fault
  (an input-blind judge) was fixable and is fixed.
