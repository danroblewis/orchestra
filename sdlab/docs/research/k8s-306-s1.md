## Cause (confirmed)

306's dispatch is `min(0.6 x queue, 40 x ready)`. The cap binds on 36% of short steps and 27% of long
steps. No linear row can express it. Every strategy put a proxy in its place (0.432 x queue, 22.8 x ready
- 4.91 x retries, 5.51 x retries), and the queue's balance absorbed the rest of the error. That broke
the traffic gain and the retries flows.

Confirmed in process before any change (scratchpad `s1/chk.py`): the queue, inflight and retries rows
regressed with the regime-clipped series `m = min(queue, 66.67 x ready)` in place of the queue level fit
the noise-free data to rounding. The numbers come out at 1.0 traffic, -0.6 m, -0.08 queue and +0.6
retries (rms 3.7e-5).

## Change (each strategy's own package; no cross-imports; no case or input-type branch)

`capmin.py` is one identical copy in each of the 43 packages (`tests/test_capmin.py` checks they match).

- **`offer`** looks for a capacity limit using the data alone. For each non-final measured stock X and
  each capacity Y (another non-counter stock, or an input):
  - theta is fitted on X's row (quantile grid of X/Y, then golden section).
  - m = MIN(X, theta Y) must take from X: its coefficient in X's row is negative.
  - Its receiver is the row where m enters positively and lowers the residual most.
  - theta is then refined on both rows together.
  - The evidence is the kink: what m adds to both rows beyond a constant, every level and input, and
    **every product of two levels or inputs**. A smooth curve is not a limit. Era columns are added per
    announced era.
  - It must exceed 6 log T, and so must m's linear gain. Each regime must hold on at least 5% of the
    steps. A residual below 1e-8 of the mean squared change counts as rounding.
  - Where all of this holds, m is added as a measured input. `opts["capmin"]` records its source and
    receiver.
- **`excluded` / `readers`**: only the rows of the limited flow (X and its receiver, or X alone for an
  outflow) may read m. The boundary's rule for measured inputs does not apply to m, which is a function
  of measured levels.
- **`restore`** writes every term that reads m back as the expression `min(a * X, a theta * Y)`, with an
  `id`.
- **`identify`** is wrapped at the end of each `__init__.py`. The wrap is idempotent if a strategy calls
  itself.

Where a strategy's own fit needed the series wired in:
- The earned refits (`earned.py` in 23 packages, `common_cause/refit.py`, `earned_pieces/__init__.py`,
  `shared_noise/identify.py`), blockwise, matrix_v2, odefit (`inside.py`, `run.py`) and sindy use
  `excluded`. Before this, the declared boundary hid m from inflight's row, and the matrix family refitted
  the move without it.
- `lna/run.py` and its 11 vendored copies (archetypes, combined2, engine_forms, gap_prior, kalman_lna,
  lna_levels/loops/products/pulses/stack, structures) offer m as one move atom from X to its receiver, or
  as a drain of X. They do not offer it as links and drains on every stock.
- matrix_v2's own `input_flows` read every input term as an outside flow. It now writes m as one move, at
  the mean of the source's and the receiver's coefficients.

The fix idea's second part needed no change. With the MIN offered, no stored 306 result keeps a gap form
reading a counter (0 rows at 6e18658/f640c89).

## What still fails, and why

- **counts (68 cells): mostly a second cause, not the MIN.** In 57 of them the MIN part is right. The
  failure is the retries split: `retries -> outside` reads about 0.34 (true 0.4) on short runs, or
  `retries -> queue` reads about 0.53 (true 0.6) on long runs, with a spurious `responses_total ->
  retries` and `retries -> inflight`.
  - retries drains all it holds each step. Under counting noise the engine caps its outflows at the
    level, so it loses E[min(N, level)] and not the full rate.
  - Least squares with the true structure reads the same bias: retries enters the queue row at 0.54 on
    counts-long (scratchpad `s1/chk.py`).
  - milp passes because it models that expectation (docs/research/milp.md).
  - This is 305's open item (k8s-305-r2.md, "Open"). It needs its own broad fix (a capped-drain
    expectation in each fit). This is a data-model limit for least squares, not the MIN.
- **counts, MIN mis-routed (11 cells):**
  - common_cause, effect_hats, held_out, inflow_effects, schedules, structures and z3exact on counts-long:
    the MIN lands on `queue -> retries` or `queue -> responses_total`.
  - effect_hats, inflow_effects and schedules on counts-short: `inflight -> errors_total` reads traffic.
  - These strategies start from the pinned `sdlab/discovery/identify.py`, which reads m in every row. Its
    outside-flow terms are then paired by each strategy's boundary pass.
  - A fix would pair m's terms inside each of those strategies before their search. Not done.
- **meas (86 cells):** the screen offers no MIN on meas-short, because the kink is lost in measurement
  error. On meas-long the rest fails as before. This is cause S2 (errors in variables).
