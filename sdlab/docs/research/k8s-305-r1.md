# Kubernetes rung 6 (case 305), broad cause R1: the give-up drain folded into the retry move

**Status:** fixed in 24 strategies (commit 427d794, 2026-10-20). Not changed: sindy and schedules (see below).

## Cause (confirmed)

`earned_joint.decide` (and `earned_pieces/joint.py`, `common_cause/joint.py`) ties a source (its drain set to what
its receivers gain, no exit) when BIC on the "counting likelihood" improves. The test was not a likelihood-ratio
test, for two reasons:

1. **Not nested.** `System.fit` refits each fit's throughputs (the variance, phi x gross) from that fit's own
   numbers, so a tie could lower -2 log L by claiming more variance. On 305 counts-long (seed 305) the free fit
   (queue row reads retries at 0.6775, retries drains 0.9511, about 4 sd apart) scored nll 60903.75 and the tied
   fit 60901.62 with one number fewer, so the tie won. Maximising that likelihood directly (scipy SLSQP, scratch
   `ml.py`) shows that the variance channel is itself misleading here: the free ML puts the retries drain at 0.84
   (true 1.0) and the tie costs only 4.8 < log T. The retries stock empties every step (drain 0.6 + 0.4 = 1), so its
   outflow is far less noisy than its throughput says.
2. **Rows taken as independent.** A move's count leaves its source and arrives in its receiver, so the two rows
   share noise. With fixed throughputs only, 305 counts-short still tied (row by row the exit is 1.9 sd, cost 3.76 <
   log T 5.70). A generalised least squares over the rows with that covariance raises it to 8.53. On 40 simulated
   counts-short seeds (scratch `eff.py`, true columns), the exit's spread is 0.179 row by row against 0.151 jointly.
   The Cramer-Rao bound is 0.116.

## Change (in each strategy's own copy, never the pinned library)

- `System.shared(fit)`: each step's noise covariance from the free fit, held fixed. The throughputs go on the
  diagonal, and each move j -> s adds minus rho x its flow between the two rows (positive definite by construction).
  rho (0..1, one per unit) is read off the free fit's residual cross-products, so rows that do not share noise
  stay independent (measurement-type noise, or a "move" that is really a link: 081-noisy-link gaps-short ties A
  wrongly when rho is forced to 1).
- `System.shared_nll(tied, W, cuts)`: all rows fitted together by GLS, with the ties as constraints and each
  row's pieces as its own levels. W is fixed, so a tie can only raise it.
- `decide`: a source with a steady inflow is tied or freed on `shared_nll` (BIC price log T per number). A source
  whose inflow changes (pieces) is judged as before, on each fit's own throughputs and pieces. Its row cannot tell
  its drain from the changes, and the throughput a tie implies is the evidence there. With the shared test for
  every source, `tests/test_ladder_earned_pieces.py::test_an_intake_that_changes_every_30_steps_keeps_its_pieces`
  failed (inbox drain 0.385, should be 0.3), and sd-pipeline counts-long gained a spurious inbox drain. This
  split is decided by a property of the fitted row (the same one `held` already uses), not by case or input type.

The same code goes into blockwise, censored, drifting_drain, hidden_transit, latent_eras, mass_balance, numopt_bo,
numopt_cma, numopt_nm, numopt_powell, odefit, own_tables, periodic_inflows, coflow_hats, loop_transit,
realistic_hats, matrix_v2, recession, starved_coflows, held_out, realistic_flows and matrix (`earned_joint.py`), and
into earned_pieces and common_cause (`joint.py`). common_cause reads every same-unit row as a receiver, as its copy
already did. An intermediate commit 6944786 (fixed throughputs only) was superseded and its stored rows are history.
