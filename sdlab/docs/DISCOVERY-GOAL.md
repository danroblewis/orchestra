# Goal: discovery that recovers the true model

This is the brief for an open-ended improvement loop on SD Lab's discovery. Read it, then
[DISCOVERY-LOOP.md](DISCOVERY-LOOP.md) (how to iterate) and
[DISCOVERY-EXPERIMENTS.md](DISCOVERY-EXPERIMENTS.md) (what has been tried, with numbers).

## The goal

Every ground-truth example (`sdlab/examples/sd-*`, each one generated by a known system dynamics
model) must be recovered well enough that the discovered model **behaves like the true one**:

> For every sd-* example, at both its default length and its long length:
> **skill@20 >= 90% and skill@100 >= 80%**

measured by `node tests/bench/evaluate.mjs --goal`, which exits 0 only when every run meets it.

skill@H is the share of the true model's movement over H steps that the found model predicts, both
started from the same states at ~150 moments and fed the same outside inflows: 100% = behaves as the
truth, 0% = no better than assuming nothing changes, below 0 = worse (it runs off on its own). Two
models that behave the same are equally good, whatever their structure looks like.

Keep going until the goal is met. Do not stop at a plateau: when one approach stalls, try a
different one (see "Ideas" below). When it is met, keep it met: lock the result in with tests.

## What you may do

Anything to discovery. Rewrite `sdlab/static/identify.js` or replace it; reuse or delete parts of
`sdlab/static/discover.js`; add equation forms, model hidden stocks, model eras, estimate outside
inflows differently, use simulation error in model selection, add a nonlinear search, ensembles,
whatever works. Change the graders only to fix a genuine bug in them (record it in the experiment
log with the evidence), never to make a number look better. Add tests freely.

## What you must not do

- **Never change a simulation.** `sdlab/sdtruth.py`, the equation functions in `sdlab/runner.py`
  (`desired_rate`, `multiplier`, `effect_value`), and every file of every `sdlab/examples/sd-*`
  example are fixed: discovery works against them. `tests/test_simulations_frozen.py` checks their
  fingerprints and must keep passing; never re-record them.
- **Never let discovery see the truth.** It gets only what the page would give it: the example's
  measured stocks (`sdlab/examples/<id>.json`: ids, names, units, final, group) and the measured
  levels over time, plus announced eras. Not `truth.model.json`, not `truth.json`, not the hidden
  levels, not the schedule. No per-example special cases or tuning keyed to an example's id or
  stock names.
- Do not break the rest: keep `node --test tests/*.mjs`, `uv run pytest -q` and
  `uv run ruff check sdlab tests` green. If the page's Discover button uses the engine you change,
  it must keep working.

## How to work

Follow DISCOVERY-LOOP.md. In short: run `node tests/bench/evaluate.mjs --label "..."` (all examples in
parallel, about a minute; compared with the last full run), pick the worst example, form one
hypothesis, check it directly (score the true structure, print a row, look at residuals), change one
thing, re-run, read every row, and **record every attempt in DISCOVERY-EXPERIMENTS.md** - what,
why, numbers before and after, kept or reverted. Save a baseline when the overall numbers reach a new
best (`--save best`). Commit nothing unless asked.

21 ground-truth examples, 42 runs (default and long lengths). **9 of 42 runs meet the goal.**
Parts 62.6%, mean skill@20 15.5%, mean skill@100 17.9% (each run clipped at -100% in the means).

- Meet it: sd-saas (both), sd-hidden-chain (both), sd-reservoirs (both), sd-hospital (long),
  sd-pipeline (long), sd-kanban (default).
- Close (70-90%): sd-ci, sd-ev-fleet, sd-restaurant, sd-manufacturing, sd-recycling (long),

- Middling (0-70%): sd-supply, sd-housing (default), sd-orders (default), sd-pipeline (default),

Compare with `--baseline start21`.

## Ideas (not instructions)

- **Announced eras** (sd-eras, sd-ci, sd-restaurant, sd-university, sd-support-tiers): rows whose
  coefficients may change at an announced era; keep structure shared, numbers per era where the
  data says so.
- **Hidden stocks** (hidden-chain, hospital lab, kanban defects, saas lapsed, ...): offer delayed
  copies of other stocks' levels (exp, erlang, pipe filters at several delays) as regressors; a
  chosen one implies a hidden stock between the two.
- **Goal-seeking and negative-coefficient links** (sd-orders, sd-supply, staffing, admissions): a
  flow whose rate falls as a stock rises (linear with negative terms, gap on a third stock); read
  the matrix's negative entries as such instead of "drains faster".
- **Runaway found models** (population, services, university): the one-step regression can pick
  coefficients whose simulated loop is unstable. Check stability of the found matrix; prefer
  models by multi-step (simulation) error; constrain signs by conservation.
- **Fast or spiky outside inputs** (kanban sprint spikes, day/night, release bursts): better
  estimates of a stock's changing inflow (robust or impulse-aware change detection, or estimating
  rows that have one from the rows that see their flows).
- **Floors at zero**: the truth's flows cannot go negative (`desired_rate` floors at 0); a linear
  fit through the floor is biased near empty stocks.
- **Short runs** (the default lengths): less data, so priors (conservation, units) matter more.
